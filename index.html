Our story

Two best friends,
six happy hives.

What began as a fun new adventure in 2023 has grown into a true passion. We proudly care for six thriving hives across Boulder, and we believe good honey and a healthy planet go hand in hand — so we spend just as much time teaching neighbors about pollinators as we do bottling honey.

Pollinator advocacy

We show up
for the bees.

Honey is just the start. We host hands-on workshops and community outreach around Boulder to help pollinators thrive — because our hives are only as healthy as the neighborhood around them.

See Upcoming Workshops
  • Free "Bee 101" workshops at local schools and libraries
  • Native & pollinator-friendly planting guides for Boulder yards
  • Hive tours and tastings for community groups
  • Partnering with local farms on pollinator habitat
