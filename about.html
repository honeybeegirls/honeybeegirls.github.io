We're Brenna
& Kaitlyn.

Two best friends who started our beekeeping journey in 2023 and quickly fell in love with our bees. What began as a fun new adventure has grown into a true passion.

How it started

From backyard curiosity
to six thriving hives.

It started with two hives and a lot of questions. Now we proudly care for six thriving hives across Boulder County — tending them through every season, harvesting raw wildflower honey, and learning something new from our bees every single week.

We value ethical beekeeping, community education, and — most importantly — having fun. Every jar we bottle carries a little bit of that spirit, and a lot of Colorado wildflowers.

The hive story so far

2023

Hives one & two

Brenna & Kaitlyn install their first two hives in Boulder and start learning the ropes of backyard beekeeping together.

2024

Growing the apiary

The hives multiply, the first big honey harvest arrives, and Honey Bee Girls starts bottling jars for friends and neighbors.

We're expanding into beeswax candles and salves — made from the same hives, with the same care.

What we stand for

Our values

Ethical Beekeeping

Our bees' health always comes first. We manage our hives thoughtfully, harvest responsibly, and never take more honey than the colony can spare.

Community Education

We believe healthier pollinator populations start with an informed neighborhood — so we teach as much as we sell.

Having Fun

Beekeeping should be joyful. If we're not smiling in our suits, we're not doing it right.

Pollinator advocacy

Workshops &
outreach

We host hands-on, beginner-friendly sessions around Boulder — no experience (or beekeeping suit) required. Bring your questions, your kids, or your whole garden club.
